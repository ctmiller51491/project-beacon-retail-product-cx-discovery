## FEATURE BENCHMARK BY CATEGORY

### 1. ACCOUNTS & PAYMENTS (Launch Phase)

**Must-Have Features:**
- ✅ Profit-Sharing Investment Accounts (5-6% p.a.) - competitive with Mashreq Neo's 6.25%
- ✅ Instant Emirates ID onboarding (<2 minutes) - table stakes in UAE
- ✅ Facial recognition + liveness detection KYC - 80%+ conversion target
- ✅ Zero-fee domestic transfers - competitive differentiator
- ✅ Free international transfers (50+ countries: GCC, India, Philippines, UK, US)
- ✅ AANI Pay integration (India-UAE instant transfers)
- ✅ Multi-currency wallets (AED, USD, EUR, GBP, SAR, INR)
- ✅ Zakat calculator + Sadaqah portal - Sharia UX essentials
- ✅ Cheque deposits via mobile app

**Best Practices:**
- Sub-accounts (minimum 10): Bills, Savings Goals, Shared Accounts, Emergency Fund
- Each sub-account with dedicated virtual card for spending control
- Round-up savings (1x-10x multipliers) + Salary Sorter automation
- Open banking integration for external account aggregation (Wave 2B)

### 2. CARDS (Launch Phase)

**Must-Have Features:**
- ✅ Instant virtual debit card upon account opening
- ✅ Physical Visa/Mastercard debit (3-5 day delivery)
- ✅ Unlimited virtual cards with merchant category controls
- ✅ Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay integration
- ✅ Instant card freeze/unfreeze
- ✅ Location-based security ("Street Mode")
- ✅ Real-time spending notifications
- ✅ ATM fee reimbursement (domestic UAE ATMs)

**Premium Tier (AED 200-250/month):**
- Metal card
- Albertine-style concierge (unlimited requests)
- 1,000+ airport lounge access (Priority Pass)
- Enhanced profit-sharing (+1-2% p.a. bonus)
- Travel insurance (AED 500K coverage)
- Cashback: 1-2% on all purchases, 5% on rotating categories
- Annual subscription bundle (The National newspaper, gym membership, streaming)

**Best Practices:**
- RevPoints-style loyalty: 1 point/AED, transfer to 30+ airlines (Emirates, Etihad, etc.)
- Single-use virtual cards for online shopping security
- Spending limits by category (dining, shopping, entertainment)
- QR code payments (compatible with UAE payment systems)

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### 1. Sharia Governance (Pre-Launch)

**Requirements:**
- Establish Sharia Supervisory Board (minimum 3 scholars) per CBUAE mandate
- Implement Shariah Compliance Function (SCF) team per April 2024 CBUAE standard
- Obtain AAOIFI certification for all financial products
- Achieve Higher Shari'ah Authority (HSA) approval
- Full compliance by April 2025 deadline

**Risk:** Delayed Sharia certification blocks product launch. Mitigation: Engage Sharia board by June 2026.

### 2. Technology Platform Selection

**Core Banking System:**
- Cloud-native, API-first architecture
- Temenos, Thought Machine, Mambu, or similar
- Must support Sharia profit-sharing calculations
- Real-time processing capabilities
- 99.95%+ uptime SLA

**Partnerships:**
- Investment platform: Sarwa, Wahed, or regional alternative with Halal screening
- Payment gateway: Network International or regional provider
- KYC/AML: UAE government database integration mandatory
- Card issuer: Visa or Mastercard with local issuing bank
- Cloud provider: AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud with UAE data residency

### 3. Regulatory Licensing

**Required Licenses:**
- DFSA banking license (digital bank category)
- CBUAE oversight registration
- Payment services license
- Investment advisory license (if offering robo-advisory)
- Crypto trading license (Wave 2+ if pursuing)
- Takaful brokerage license (for embedded insurance)

**Timeline:** 12-18 months for banking license; initiate by Q2 2026 for March 2027 launch.

### 4. Customer Acquisition Strategy

- Partnership launches: Employers (salary transfer offers), universities (student accounts)

**CAC Target:** AED 150-200 blended (60% lower for referred customers)

**Retention Strategy:**
- Onboarding excellence (80%+ conversion, <2 min)
- Early engagement: First transaction within 24 hours (instant virtual card)
- Habit formation: Salary transfer incentives (higher profit-sharing rates)
- Switching assistance: Auto-transfer standing orders from old bank

### 5. Talent & Team

**Key Hires:**

## RISKS & MITIGATIONS

### High Risks

1. **Sharia Certification Delays**
   - Impact: Product launch delay, competitive disadvantage
   - Mitigation: Engage Sharia board Q2 2026; parallel track AAOIFI standards review

2. **Banking License Delays**
   - Impact: Cannot launch March 2027
   - Mitigation: Apply by Q2 2026; explore partnership with licensed bank for early launch

3. **Competitive Response (Mashreq/Liv. launch Sharia products)**
   - Impact: Loss of differentiation
   - Mitigation: Speed to market; premium tier and embedded finance as additional differentiators

### Medium Risks

4. **Technology Integration Complexity**
   - Impact: Launch delays, poor user experience
   - Mitigation: Select proven vendors (Temenos, Mambu); allocate 6 months for integration testing

5. **Partnership Negotiations (Investment platform, BNPL, Travel)**
   - Impact: Reduced feature set at launch
   - Mitigation: Initiate discussions Q2 2026; have backup partners identified

6. **Customer Acquisition Cost Higher Than Expected**
   - Impact: Profitability timeline extension
   - Mitigation: Sovereign capital backing allows runway; focus on viral referrals; partnership launches

### Low Risks

7. **App Store Rating Volatility**
   - Impact: Reputation damage, reduced downloads
   - Mitigation: Exceptional UX from launch; careful change management; rapid customer support
